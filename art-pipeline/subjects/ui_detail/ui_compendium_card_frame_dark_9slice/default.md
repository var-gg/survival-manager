# UI Detail Asset: Compendium dark card frame 9-slice

```prompt
# UI detail asset — dark compendium card frame, 9-slice ready

Create a single reusable 9-slice card frame asset for a dark anime fantasy RPG System Compendium UI.

The attached Compendium mockup is the style reference only. Do not reproduce the whole screen. Create only one isolated UI frame asset.

## Component

Compact square card frame, centered on a 1024x1024 canvas.

Visual:
- Very thin dark navy / charcoal frame material.
- Antique warm-gold hairline outer trim.
- Subtle painterly bevel on the frame only.
- Tiny corner cap details, restrained and buildable.
- Very subtle inner highlight line.
- Extremely compact border, suitable for small 180-240px UI cards.
- The frame must look like a thin decorative stroke, not a thick modal panel.
- Premium codex/card feeling, not bright parchment.

## 9-slice requirements

- The visible frame border should occupy only about 20-28px from each edge of the card shape.
- Leave about 64px safe margin between the outer frame and the canvas edge.
- The center of the frame must be pure #FF00FF chroma so it becomes transparent after cutout.
- The outside of the frame must also be pure #FF00FF chroma.
- Corners may be lightly ornamented, but keep them small. Do not use giant corner plates.
- The middle stretches of each side must be clean, thin, and repeatable.
- No decorative horizontal rails crossing the center. No long internal bars that would overlap text when sliced.
- No wide dark metal band. No thick top/bottom rail. No large modal-style border.
- No glow. No selected state. No drop shadow outside the frame.

## Chroma discipline

Everything outside the actual frame and inside the transparent center hole must be pure #FF00FF. No blur, no haze, no semi-transparent pixels, no particles, no shadow on the chroma.

## Negative

No full UI screen. No text. No glyph labels. No skill icon. No character. No item art. No parchment fill. No bright beige center. No selected glow. No heavy fantasy carving. No thick modal frame. No internal decorative lines across the transparent center.
```
